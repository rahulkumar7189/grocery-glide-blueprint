# FreshKeeper: Grocery Management System MVP

## Overview
FreshKeeper is a comprehensive home grocery management system designed to help you track inventory, manage shopping lists, and analyze your grocery spending patterns. This MVP provides essential features for efficient household grocery management.

## Features

### 🏠 Home
Your central dashboard for quick access to all FreshKeeper features:
- Overview of current inventory status
- Recent shopping activity
- Quick actions and shortcuts
- Personalized recommendations

### 📦 Inventory
Keep track of what you have in your pantry, fridge, and freezer:
- Add, edit, and remove items from your inventory
- Track expiration dates to reduce food waste
- Set low-stock alerts for frequently used items
- Categorize items for easy organization
- Search and filter functionality

### 🛒 Shopping
Streamline your grocery shopping experience:
- Create and manage multiple shopping lists
- Add items from inventory or manually
- Check off items as you shop
- Share lists with family members
- Organize items by store aisle or category
- Import common shopping items

### 📊 Analytics
Gain insights into your grocery spending and consumption patterns:
- View spending trends over time
- Track most purchased items
- Analyze food waste patterns
- Budget tracking and alerts
- Category-wise expense breakdown
- Generate custom reports
