# Inventory Module

## Overview
The Inventory module helps you track all grocery items in your home, including items in your pantry, refrigerator, and freezer.

## Features
- **Item Management**: Add, edit, and remove items from your inventory
- **Expiration Tracking**: Monitor expiration dates to reduce food waste
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Get notified when items are running low
- **Categories**: Organize items by categories (Dairy, Produce, Meat, Grains, etc.)
- **Search & Filter**: Quickly find items in your inventory
- **Quantity Tracking**: Keep track of quantities and units

## Implementation Details
This module will contain:
- Data models for inventory items
- CRUD operations for managing inventory
- Expiration date alerts and notifications
- Category management
- Search and filtering functionality

## Future Enhancements
- Barcode scanning for quick item addition
- Integration with shopping module
- Photo upload for items
- Batch import/export functionality
